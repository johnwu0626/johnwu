1. 存放在你裝置上的資料

為了讓你下次能接續遊玩，遊戲會把以下內容存在 App 的本機儲存空間，不會上傳到任何伺服器：

  • 機台進度：持有金幣、投入與賞球統計、保留與獎球狀態、王印、熔核連鍛、SUPER BONUS、工坊等級與典藏、王庫遠征，以及大獎影片的播放位置。
  • 偏好設定：聲音開關、減少動態效果、畫面品質與發射力道。
  • 遊戲版本更新前自動保留的進度備份。

解除安裝 App，或在系統設定中「清除儲存空間」，都會刪除這些資料，且無法由我們復原。若你的 Android 裝置開啟了系統備份，Android 可能依 Google 的備份機制把本 App 的資料（含上述進度）備份到你的 Google 帳戶；此功能由你的裝置設定與 Google 控制。

App 版進度與網頁版分開保存，不會自動互通。

2. 匯出備份

在「機台設定」按「匯出備份」時，Android 會開啟系統的儲存位置選擇器，由你決定檔案存放位置。檔案（golden-forge-save.json）只包含上述遊戲進度，我們不會收到這個檔案。

3. 網路與權限

遊戲的圖片、影片與音效全部內建於 App，遊玩時不需要連網，也不會傳送資料。App 套件中的網路權限（INTERNET）來自 App 所使用的 Capacitor 框架預設設定，本遊戲沒有使用它下載或上傳資料。只有在你按下「開啟完整政策」時，才會用你的瀏覽器開啟本頁。

4. 虛擬金幣與遊戲內容

本遊戲是模擬柏青哥機台的娛樂遊戲，含有隨機抽選與獎勵演出。金幣只用於遊戲內，無法以真實金錢購買，也不能兌換現金、點數、商品或任何具實際價值的獎品。本遊戲不以兒童為對象。

Dwarven Gold Forge — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2, 2026 · Applies to the Android app (com.johnwu0626.dwarvengoldforge)

Summary
  • No sign-up or login. We run no servers and do not collect or upload any personal data.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your own device.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • In-game coins are virtual numbers. They cannot be bought and cannot be exchanged for cash, credits or goods.

1. Data stored on your device

So you can continue where you left off, the game keeps the following in the app's local storage and never uploads it:

  • Machine progress: coin balance, play statistics, reserved and bonus balls, royal seals, forge chains, SUPER BONUS, workshop levels and collection, vault expeditions, and the playback position of jackpot films.
  • Preferences: sound, reduced motion, graphics quality and launch power.
  • Automatic progress backups kept before a game update changes the save format.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in system settings deletes this data, and we cannot restore it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may back up this app's data (including your progress) to your Google Account through Google's backup service; this is controlled by your device settings and Google.

Progress in the app is separate from the web version and is not shared between them.

2. Exporting a backup

"匯出備份" (Export backup) in the machine settings opens Android's file picker so you choose where the file is saved. The file (golden-forge-save.json) contains only the progress above; it is never sent to us.

3. Network and permissions

All images, films and sounds are bundled with the app. Playing needs no connection and sends no data. The INTERNET permission in the package is a default of the Capacitor framework the app is built with; the game does not use it to download or upload data. This page opens in your browser only when you tap "開啟完整政策" (Open full policy).

4. Virtual coins and game content

This is an entertainment game that simulates a pachinko machine, with random draws and reward presentations. Coins exist only inside the game and cannot be bought with real money or exchanged for cash, credits, goods or any prize of real-world value. The game is not directed at children.

5. Third-party services

The app contains no advertising, analytics, sign-in or cloud-save services. When you download or update the app through Google Play, Google processes related data, such as install and crash statistics, under its own policies; we do not collect that data.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated. Questions: johnwu0626@gmail.com.
