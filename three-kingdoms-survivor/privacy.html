《無雙割草：三國奇兵》隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 18 日｜適用：Android App（com.johnwu0626.threekingdomssurvivor）與網頁版

繁體中文English
重點摘要
  • 本遊戲不需要註冊或登入，我們沒有自己的伺服器，也不會收集你的姓名、Email、電話或位置。
  • 遊戲進度只存在你自己的裝置上。
  • Android App 內的「激勵廣告」由 Google AdMob 提供，Google 會依其政策處理廣告識別碼等資料。

1. 我們存放在你裝置上的資料

為了讓你下次能接續遊玩，遊戲會把以下內容存在裝置本機（瀏覽器／App 的 localStorage），不會上傳到我們的任何伺服器：

  • 遠征自動存檔：目前陣數、所選名將、神兵、翠玉與戰力等。
  • 局外養成：將魂數量、兵法閣天賦等級、已解鎖名將。
  • 最高紀錄陣數。

解除安裝 App、在系統設定中「清除儲存空間」，或清除瀏覽器的網站資料，都會一併刪除這些資料，且無法復原。若你的 Android 裝置開啟了系統備份，Android 可能會依 Google 的備份機制把本 App 的資料（含上述進度）備份到你的 Google 帳號，此功能由你的裝置設定與 Google 控制。

2. 廣告（僅 Android App）

Android App 只在你主動點選「華佗顯靈・原地復活」「免費重鑄補給」「雙倍領取將魂」時播放激勵廣告，看完即發放對應的遊戲內獎勵。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供；依 Google 公開的說明，Google Mobile Ads SDK 會自動收集下列資料，用於投放廣告、成效分析與防範詐欺，傳輸過程以 TLS 加密：

資料類型內容
IP 位址可用來推估裝置的大致地區
裝置識別碼Android 廣告 ID、App set ID
產品互動例如廣告的顯示、點擊、影片觀看
診斷資訊例如 App／SDK 的啟動時間、效能與耗電

位於歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士的玩家，App 會先透過 Google 的同意管理工具（UMP）詢問你是否同意個人化廣告。你也可以隨時在 Android 系統設定的「廣告」選項（依機型位於「隱私權」或「Google」底下）重設或刪除廣告 ID。Google 如何使用這些資料，請見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式中的資訊與 Google 隱私權政策。網頁版不顯示廣告。

3. 網路字型

遊戲畫面使用 Google Fonts（思源宋體／黑體等）。裝置連網時會向 Google 的字型伺服器下載字型檔，Google 因此會收到你的 IP 位址與瀏覽器／WebView 資訊，詳見 Google Fonts 隱私權說明。離線時遊戲改用裝置內建字型，仍可正常遊玩。

4. 我們不收集的資料

  • 姓名、Email、電話號碼、帳號密碼
  • 精確位置、聯絡人、相機、麥克風、相片或檔案
  • 付款資訊（本遊戲沒有應用程式內購買）

5. 兒童隱私

本遊戲並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們也不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。若你認為有兒童的資料經由本遊戲被收集，請依第 7 節聯絡我們。

6. 政策更新

若本政策有變更，我們會更新本頁與上方的生效日期；重大變更會在 App 更新說明中註明。

Privacy Policy — 無雙割草：三國奇兵

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Applies to the Android app (com.johnwu0626.threekingdomssurvivor) and the web version of the game.

Summary
  • No sign-up or login. We run no servers and do not collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Game progress stays on your device.
  • Rewarded ads in the Android app are served by Google AdMob, which processes data such as the advertising ID under Google's policies.

1. Data stored on your device

To let you resume play, the game keeps the following in local storage on your device. It is never uploaded to any server of ours: your current campaign save (stage, chosen hero, weapons, jade and power), meta progression (souls, talent levels, unlocked heroes), and your best stage. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage or clearing the browser's site data deletes it permanently. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up the app's data (including this progress) to your Google account; this is controlled by your device settings and Google.

2. Advertising (Android app only)

The Android app shows a rewarded ad only when you tap one of the three reward buttons (revive in place, free reroll, double souls), and grants the in-game reward after you finish watching. Ads are served by Google AdMob. According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects the following for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, encrypted in transit with TLS: IP address (which can estimate approximate location), device identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID), product interactions (such as ad views, taps and video views) and diagnostic information (such as launch time, performance and energy use).

Players in the EEA, UK or Switzerland are first asked for consent to personalised ads through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Ads section of Android Settings (under Privacy or Google, depending on the device). See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The web version shows no ads.

3. Web fonts

The game uses Google Fonts. When online, your device downloads font files from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and browser/WebView information (see the Google Fonts privacy FAQ). Offline, the game falls back to built-in fonts and remains playable.

4. Data we do not collect

Name, email, phone number or passwords; precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files; payment information (the game has no in-app purchases).

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal data. If you believe a child's data has been collected through the game, contact us as described below.

6. Changes

We will update this page and the effective date above when the policy changes; significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
